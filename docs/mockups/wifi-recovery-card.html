Wi-Fi loss recovery, rebuilt in the Cosy design language

Same philosophy as the overhauled Cosy Octopus plan card: one state-coloured hero that says what is happening now, one progress rail that shows where the recovery is, controls that are reachable, and every diagnostic demoted to collapsible detail. Every panel below is rendered from the real entity attributes the integration publishes — including the guarded failure, cooldown and authentication states. Nothing about the recovery policy changes: one verified 6 ↔ 11 channel change per outage, a one-hour cooldown, and an explicit opt-in.

What moved, and why

StatusA pill in the corner becomes the hero headline in the current state colour — green armed, amber waiting, red failed, grey off.
Eight metricsReduced to two facts in the hero foot plus a failure pip meter. Router, cooldown, verification window and history move into one details block.
TimingISO strings such as 2026-09-16T13:38:00+00:00 become a live countdown with a clock time, the way the Cosy card shows its next charge.
FlowThe policy sentence becomes a four-step rail: watch, wait, switch, verify. Blocked and completed steps are visible.
ControlsThe full-width button and raw number input become a pill switch and a stepped value matching the Cosy control rows, still 0–60 minutes.
IsolationThe card moves into a shadow root. Today it injects bare h2 / p / button rules into the whole dashboard.
